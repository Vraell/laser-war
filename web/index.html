How to Play

Place

Choose / or \ and place one mirror on a highlighted empty square.

Fire

Both side lasers fire after every move. Mirrors turn a beam by 90 degrees.

Damage

A beam destroys the first shield it touches. Mirrors are permanent.

Win

Hit the opposing king without hitting your own. A simultaneous hit is a draw.

Restrictions

Mirrors cannot touch a king. Both kings and both lasers must retain a possible path.

Controls

Q and E select mirrors. U undoes. R restarts. Escape pauses.
